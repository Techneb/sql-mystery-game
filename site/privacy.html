What the paper knows about you

Privacy notice — last updated 10 October 2026

The Ritz Affair is a browser game for learning SQL. It has no accounts. The whole game, database included, runs in your browser. The page shows advertising from Google AdSense in one box beside the game, under the schema, never inside a chapter. When you first visit, Google's consent message asks whether Google and its partners may use cookies and your data for advertising and for usage statistics (below). The game itself sets no cookies.

Investigate (learning mode)

Your progress, notes, queries, badges and day/night choice are saved in your browser's local storage (ritz.learn, ritz.compete, ritz.theme) so you can pick up where you left off. Only you can see them; clearing this site's data in your browser erases them. None of it is sent anywhere.

Your consent: Google's consent message

On your first visit, a message from Google (Google's certified consent management platform, served by the AdSense script) asks for your consent to advertising and usage statistics, with buttons to accept or refuse and a way to manage each purpose. Google keeps your choice in its own cookies on this site. Until you choose, and whenever you refuse, the page tells Google's tags that every purpose is denied (Google Consent Mode: ad storage, ad user data, ad personalisation and analytics storage all start as denied).

You can change your mind at any time: the Privacy and cookie settings link at the bottom of the game's front page reopens Google's message. If you withdraw consent to statistics, the page deletes the _ga cookies.

Visitors outside the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are not shown the message: for them every purpose stays denied, usage statistics never load, and the ads shown are not personalised.

Usage statistics (only with your consent)

Only if you consent to usage statistics (analytics storage) in Google's message does the page load Google Analytics 4:

  • It sets the first-party cookies _ga and _ga_<id>, which Google keeps for up to 13 months by default. The collected data itself is kept for 14 months, then deleted.
  • Google receives page views: the page address and referrer, and your browser, device model and approximate location (country, region, city) as Google Analytics works them out from the connection. Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses.
  • And these game events, with only these fields:
    • chapter_open: chapter number, mode (learn or compete);
    • chapter_solve: chapter number, mode, seconds taken, number of queries, number of wrong answers;
    • game_finish: part, mode;
    • badge: badge name.

Never sent to Google: your team name or pseudo, your answers, your SQL queries, your notes.

Why: to improve the course — which chapters students drop out of, how long each takes, and to tune the difficulty and the ranks. Google signals and ad personalisation are off, and the data is not used for advertising. Google processes it on the course owner's behalf, and may do so in the United States under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Advertising

The page loads Google AdSense, which shows ads in one box beside the game, under the schema, never inside a chapter; you can fold the box away (the page remembers it in ritz.ads). The AdSense script loads on every visit, because it also carries Google's consent message; what it may do depends on your choice:

  • If you consent, Google and its partners may set and read advertising cookies and use data about your visits to this site and other sites to show personalised ads, and measure them.
  • If you refuse, or are not asked, you see non-personalised ads, chosen from the page's content and your approximate location rather than your browsing; Google may still use cookies or similar identifiers for frequency capping, aggregated reporting and fighting fraud and abuse, as its policies allow, or show no ad at all.
  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site or to other sites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising in Google's Ads Settings, or opt out of other vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info.

To withdraw or change your advertising consent, use the Privacy and cookie settings link at the bottom of the game's front page, which reopens Google's message. How Google uses data from sites that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Compete

When your teacher gives you a season link and you press Start the clock, the game sends the following to the class leaderboard, at the start, after each chapter and at the finish:

  • the pseudo you type,
  • the season number and the chapter you reached,
  • how many queries you ran and wrong answers you gave,
  • your browser's clock reading at each message and, at the finish, the time you took by that clock.

Each answer you submit in compete mode is also sent, in its simplified form, so the leaderboard can check it against the season's answers; it is checked and not stored. The leaderboard adds the time it received each message and gives your browser a code tied to your pseudo, so that nobody else can post under it afterwards. It never receives your queries or your notes. It is a Google Sheet belonging to the course teacher, reached through Google Apps Script, so Google processes it under its own terms. Anyone with a leaderboard link sees the pseudos and times of the season that link names; the teacher sees every season. The rows stay until the teacher deletes them. This is separate from usage statistics and does not depend on your answer to the consent message.

Choose a pseudo that is not your full name. A first name or a nom de guerre is enough.

What the page loads from elsewhere

  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may log your IP address.
  • Fonts come from Google Fonts, and the SQL engine (sql.js) from cdnjs; both see your IP address when your browser fetches them.
  • On every visit: Google's AdSense and consent scripts from pagead2.googlesyndication.com and fundingchoicesmessages.google.com, and the ads themselves from Google's ad servers, including googleads.g.doubleclick.net and tpc.googlesyndication.com; these see your IP address, and with your consent set advertising cookies.
  • Only if you consent to usage statistics: the Google Analytics script from googletagmanager.com, and its measurements sent to google-analytics.com.

Questions, or removing your rows

Write to contact@alephb.uk: to ask what is stored about your pseudo, or to have your leaderboard rows removed.

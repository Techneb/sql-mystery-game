What the paper knows about you

Privacy notice — last updated 30 September 2026

The Ritz Affair is a browser game for learning SQL. It has no accounts, sets no cookies, and runs no analytics, advertising or trackers. The whole game, database included, runs in your browser.

Investigate (learning mode)

Nothing leaves your browser. Your progress, notes, queries, badges and day/night choice are saved in your browser's local storage so you can pick up where you left off. Only you can see them; clearing this site's data in your browser erases them.

Compete

When your teacher gives you a season link and you press Start the clock, the game sends the following to the class leaderboard, at the start, after each chapter and at the finish:

  • the pseudo you type,
  • the season number and the chapter you reached,
  • how many queries you ran and wrong answers you gave.

The leaderboard adds the time it received each message. It never receives your queries, your answers or your notes. It is a Google Sheet belonging to the course teacher, reached through Google Apps Script, so Google processes it under its own terms. Anyone with the leaderboard link sees the pseudos and times of that season. The rows stay until the teacher deletes them.

Choose a pseudo that is not your full name. A first name or a nom de guerre is enough.

What the page loads from elsewhere

  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may log your IP address.
  • Fonts come from Google Fonts, and the SQL engine (sql.js) from cdnjs; both see your IP address when your browser fetches them.

Questions, or removing your rows

Write to contact@alephb.uk: to ask what is stored about your pseudo, or to have your leaderboard rows removed.

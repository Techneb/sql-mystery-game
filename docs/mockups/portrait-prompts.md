# Suspect portrait prompts

For `site/portraits/*.jpg`. Generate **square** (1:1), then centre-crop to 3:4 and resize to 450x600, clear of
any generator mark (see the spec's section 8). Paste the shared style, then the sitter. Priority: the three the
course owner asked to redo (Ashcombe, Sernine, Ortega read as the same lean, dark, moustached man).

## Shared style (prefix every prompt)

> Portrait painting in the style of Sir John Lavery, Paris 1912, oil on canvas, loose confident brushwork,
> rich warm colour, soft window light from the left, plain dark warm background, head and shoulders, the
> sitter looking slightly away from the viewer, painterly surface but a highly detailed, lifelike face,
> square composition with the face centred and space around the head, no frame, no text, no signature.

Negative prompt (where the tool takes one): *frame, gold frame, border, painting on a wall, text, watermark,
signature, modern clothing, photograph, cartoon, two people.*

## Sitters

**Lord Ashcombe** (redo; the neighbour, ch2-3, ch9) -- an English peer of about 58, heavy-set and ruddy-faced,
thinning white hair, white mutton-chop whiskers and a clean-shaven chin, a monocle on a black ribbon, pale blue
eyes, a faintly aggrieved expression, black frock coat, high wing collar, grey silk cravat with a pearl pin, a
gold watch chain.

**Paul Sernine** (redo; a regular, ch10) -- a Frenchman of about 30, slim, fair hair neatly parted at the side,
clean-shaven, boyish face, amused half-smile, light grey three-piece suit, white carnation in the buttonhole,
soft grey felt hat held in one hand, the ease of a man who never pays for anything.

**Raul Ortega** (redo; the R. wire in ch7, champagne in ch8) -- an Argentine cattle baron of about 50, stout and
broad-shouldered, deeply tanned, bald crown with black hair at the sides, full black beard streaked with grey,
heavy dark eyebrows, cream linen suit, a wide panama hat on his knee, a large gold signet ring engraved with an
R, a showman's confident pose.

**Horace Velmont** (ch11) -- a French society painter of about 40, lean, dark hair worn long over the collar, a
narrow pointed beard, tired eyes of a man up all night, loose dark velvet jacket with a smear of paint on the
cuff, a soft floppy bow tie.

**Ines de Almagro** (ch10) -- a Spanish widow of about 45, elegant, black silk mourning dress with a high lace
collar, black lace mantilla over dark hair greying at the temples, jet earrings, a cameo brooch, a composed,
watchful expression, a closed fan in a gloved hand.

**Rupert Blakeney** (Lupin; ch8, ch10, Part I ending) -- an English gentleman of about 35, handsome,
clean-shaven, dark hair neatly combed, a knowing, amused look, an impeccable dark overcoat with a velvet collar
(the "excellent coat"), white silk scarf, top hat in hand.

**Comtesse de Cagliostro** (Part II ending, not a suspect; already generated, `comtesse.jpg`) -- a woman of
about 30, pale, dark hair in a low loose chignon with a few strands free, red lips, a faint knowing half-smile, a
sapphire-blue silk and lace gown off the shoulders, a high black lace collar, long drop earrings.

In situ, night edition

Part II, chapter XI
Chapter XI — LAG() OVER

The Silence

Every suite makes noise at night: the lift boy writes down who rings, room service writes down who is thirsty, the telegraph desk writes down who wires, and charges it to the suite. Put the three ledgers together for the night of the 18th, suite by suite, in order of time. Somewhere on the second floor a suite fell silent at 02:05, when the lift went down, and woke at 03:10, when it came back up.
Objective
Combine lift_log, room_service and telegram for 19120518 before 06:00 into one list of (suite, time). With LAG(time) OVER (PARTITION BY suite ORDER BY time), find the suite that has an event at or before 02:05 and its next at or after 03:10. Answer: the suite number.

5

Three ways to draw the same prop

pick one; it applies to all twelve

A. Engraved line

Single-weight ink lines, a hint of hatching. Closest to what a 1912 paper printed. Recolours trivially. The mock above uses this. Cheapest to produce: hand-drawn SVG, no external assets.

B. Flat silhouette

Solid ink shapes, like a woodcut or a stencil. Reads at 24 px on the case board. Less period, more poster. Same cost as A.

C. Line plus red wash

Engraved line with one translucent accent wash (red by day, sapphire by night). The one detail that matters gets the colour. Slightly more work per prop; strongest on the chapter header.

6

Decisions I need from you

  1. Style. A, B or C above. My recommendation: C for the chapter header, with the same drawings reduced to A on the case board where they are small.
  2. Assets. Hand-drawn SVG (what this mock is, zero external files, ASCII, recolourable) or commissioned or generated raster art (richer, but each image is a file, a colour, and a Part II variant to maintain). I recommend SVG; it also prints on the certificate.
  3. Portraits. Silhouettes only (as here), so no student can read guilt from a face, or real engraved portraits per suspect. If portraits, does Blakeney get a face at all before chapter VIII?
  4. Night switch. Whole page turns at the Part II code (my proposal), or only the story column, keeping the terminal and ERD constant for the students' comfort.
  5. Board. Replace the current index cards with this pinboard, or keep the cards and add the board as an "Enlarge" view like the ERD. The pinboard needs about 380 px of width to read; on phones it would stack under the story.
  6. Thread. Solve order only (simple, always correct), or let students drag cards and draw their own threads (a notepad in pictures; more code, saved in localStorage).
  7. Props that touch the answer. The trunk shows a label, the plate shows a prefix, the account card shows a number. All are things the student has already found when the card pins. Confirm you are happy with the board showing solved answers in the clear (it does today, as text).
  8. Masthead date. Advance to 19 May for Part II, or keep 18 May throughout.

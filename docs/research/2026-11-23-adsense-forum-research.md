# AdSense "Low value content": what got sites approved (research, 2026-11-23)

## Method and limits

- Web search plus page fetches. **Reddit (all subs) and BlackHatWorld refuse this agent's fetcher (403 / domain blocked)**, and Google AdSense Community threads render by JavaScript, so their bodies could not be read directly. Where those sources are cited, the claim comes from the search engine's summary of the thread. Treat them as second-hand.
- Most "how to fix low value content" pages are vendor or SEO blogs with no first-hand case. I left them out, or marked them as vendor claims.
- First-hand reports of approval after repeated low-value refusals are rare. Most posts that promise one end with "will update after I reapply". That is a finding in itself: nobody has a reliable recipe.

Confidence labels: **High** = Google's own docs, or 3+ independent reports. **Medium** = 2 reports, or one Product Expert answer. **Low** = a single anecdote.

---

## What Google itself says (the only hard ground)

- **Review is site-wide, and redirects count as a navigation problem.** Google's "site isn't ready" help page lists the navigation issues: "redirects, pages behind a login, broken links, excessive pop-ups, and unlaunched pages". It says "Make sure that your site is fully built and launched", that content "should contain complete sentences and paragraphs, not only headlines", and that reviewers may look at all pages, not only the sign-up page. https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/81904 (current page, read 2026-11-23)
- **Low value content guidance.** Google asks for enough unique content to tell what the site is about, and "substantial value and originality" compared with similar sites. It also says to consolidate similar pages or expand them, to cut long repeated text across pages, to keep navigation, links and layout working, and to update regularly. https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/10015918 (read 2026-11-23)
- **Navigation bar.** "Your navigation bar should help users quickly understand how to interact with your site", and drop-downs must work. https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/7299563
- **Policy: "Google-served ads on screens without publisher-content".** No ads on screens "without publisher content or with low-value content, under construction, or used for alerts, navigation or other behavioral purposes". Ad serving may be limited or disabled until the screen is updated. https://support.google.com/publisherpolicies/answer/11112688
- **Ad code before approval is expected.** Units stay blank until review completes. Code on the signed-up site is needed for the review to proceed. "Test pages that are empty except for the code won't be approved." https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/2708537 , https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/7584263 , https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/81904
- **Timing.** Review "usually takes a few days, but in some cases can take 2-4 weeks". Do not remove and re-add the site, "this can delay the process". https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/9128461
- **Subdomains.** Since 2023-03-20, AdSense manages sites at the domain level only. Subdomain entries were removed and their controls promoted to the domain. https://www.seroundtable.com/google-adsense-sites-management-changes-34942.html (2023), https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/12170421
- **Insufficient content (older Google blog, 2017-03-01).** Sites made mostly of images, video or Flash, or a template with little text, get refused. Google reads keywords, word frequency and font size to match ads. https://blog.google/products/adsense/how-to-address-insufficient-content/

## Q2. App, game and tool sites

- **Product Expert view: tools rarely pass the automated first review.** In a community thread from about late 2024 or 2025, a Diamond Product Expert said "'Tools' and 'Free hosting' sites just won't make it through the bots", and that content mostly on subdomains won't work. The community guide adds that human review is mostly by invitation, for sites around 300,000 monthly uniques. https://support.google.com/adsense/thread/381630736/site-rejected-for-low-value-content?hl=en , community guide above. Confidence: Medium (Product Expert opinion, not policy).
- **Game sites get "Screens without publisher content" plus thin content.** In a 2026-11-14 newsletter roundup, a daily word game (PEGDLE) was refused for exactly those two reasons, with no fix found. Another game domain was refused three times despite a game wiki. A browser-game directory with text pages was approved. https://averageusers.substack.com/p/your-game-works-your-ad-revenue-is . Confidence: Medium (several separate reports, and it matches the policy wording).
- **The fixes people report for tool and app sites:**
  - Hand-written guide pages around the tool. The ramrom.io case wrote three guides of about 800 words, with honest limits and an About page. The outcome is implied, not stated. https://dev.to/mobeenahmedgondal/writing-guides-that-admit-the-limits-the-content-that-fixed-my-adsense-rejection-10dc
  - Ads only on screens with real content, never on empty or loading screens. A music-app pull request fixed the policy flag this way. https://github.com/Dessy71/songPlayer1/pull/58
  - A text section or blog alongside the tool. In the Webmatrices thread, a separate blog section of 15-17 articles "still works". FAQ blocks gave mixed results. https://webmatrices.com/post/then-vs-now-google-served-ads-on-screens-without-publisher-content-102
  - Confidence: Medium.
- **Crawlability of JS pages.** It is unclear whether the AdSense crawler runs JS like Googlebot (Merj's write-up on an AdSense rendering bug; vendor guides). Content present in the HTML source is safe. Content inside `<details>` is in the source but collapsed. Nothing found says collapsed text is discounted for AdSense. https://merj.com/blog/discovering-and-diagnosing-a-google-adsense-rendering-bug . Confidence: Low.

## Q3. Ad code and ad units before approval

- Google expects the code before review. Units show blank until approval (Google help, above). No source found says ad code by itself causes a refusal. Confidence: High that the head code is harmless.
- **Caveat that matters for us:** a review checks against the "screens without publisher-content" policy. An ad unit placed on an app screen whose prose is collapsed is the exact pattern behind the game refusals above. One guide advises against configuring Auto ads before approval, since that is a post-approval feature. https://yourdigihelp.com/google-adsense-approval-guide/ . Confidence: Low-Medium. Since the units are blank until approval anyway, taking them off app screens costs nothing.

## Q4. Subdomains with thin pages

- Officially, sites are managed and verified at domain level (2023). A Product Expert answer says the decision is made on the root or www. Once the root is approved, subdomains can serve ads with the same publisher code (WebmasterWorld, Brett_Tabke, 2025-08-12). An unconfirmed rumour says very different subdomains may be reviewed separately. https://www.webmasterworld.com/google_adsense/5120321.htm
- A Product Expert said "content primarily lodged on subdomains won't work", in a thread about a refused site whose content sat on subdomains, one of them behind a login. https://support.google.com/adsense/thread/381630736/site-rejected-for-low-value-content?hl=en
- Hosting-platform subdomains (*.vercel.app, *.github.io) are refused. A custom domain was necessary but not sufficient: the 2026 morinaga sites were refused again for "valuable inventory". https://dev.to/morinaga/why-google-adsense-will-not-approve-a-vercelapp-site-110b , https://dev.to/morinaga/why-im-abandoning-adsense-on-two-sites-and-betting-on-affiliate-monetization-19cb
- **No first-hand report found where a thin subdomain caused a root-domain refusal, or where removing one fixed it.** The best reasoning comes from Google's own list: redirects and thin pages are navigation and content problems "reviewers may look at" across all pages. A 16-word "has moved" page that redirects is both. Confidence: Low (inference, not evidence).

## Q5. Wait between requests

- Google: review takes a few days, up to 2-4 weeks; removing and re-adding a site delays it (help 9128461). There is no published cooldown. One guide claims "no waiting period or limit".
- The consensus is 2-4 weeks after real changes, and only once Search Console shows them indexed. One guide warns that rapid re-applications may be auto-rejected. That warning is unverified. https://cheksite.com/guides/adsense-low-value-content , https://monetizationguy.com/articles/adsense-low-value-content-the-real-fix-not-just-more-words
- Resubmitting unchanged sometimes works: "many people get approved on attempt #2 or #3 with zero changes" (dev.to bdubs, 2025-12-30; BHW posters adding "an article or two"). It also often fails, as in the 2025 six-refusals case. Confidence: Medium that quick resubmits do not cause harm by themselves, and Medium that they rarely help.

## Q6. Myths the forums say do not matter

- **A minimum number of posts or words.** Google publishes none. Approvals are reported at 5-10 posts (BHW) and refusals at 42-46 posts (2025 case). Padding word counts failed in the 2025 case. Confidence: High.
- **Domain age as a rule.** The old six-month rule was a China/India requirement, dropped around 2019. The "6 months" in today's help is about finishing account setup. Age may still correlate through traffic and trust. https://theguidex.com/resources/google-adsense-approval . Confidence: Medium.
- **Privacy, About and Contact pages as the fix.** They are needed, but rewriting them alone did not get approval in the first-hand cases (2025-06 case; Digital Point 2025-11 thread: "focusing on About Us when the site has no content"). Confidence: Medium.
- **Tag counts and SEO tweaks.** Cutting tags to 8 did nothing in the 2025 case. Confidence: Low.
- **Human review on request.** Mostly by invitation, for large sites (community guide). Confidence: Medium.

## Ranked actions for alephb.uk

1. **Wait until the new content is indexed before the fifth request.** Pages indexed "only days ago" are the strongest reason to hold. Request when Search Console shows most of the 2026-11-22 pages indexed and Google has recrawled the home page. That likely means the planned 6-13 Dec or a little later; do not go earlier. Never remove and re-add the site. (Google help 9128461, and the consensus of the guides.)
2. **Take the visible ad units off app screens until approval.** That means the game page and the exercises workbench. Keep the head script, meta tag and ads.txt for verification. Units are blank until approval anyway. This removes the "screens without publisher-content" pattern behind the game refusals above. (Policy 11112688; game reports 2026-11-14.)
3. **Make the alephb.uk home page and menu the showcase.** The root/www is what gets judged, and reviewers sample the front page and a few links. The home page needs real paragraphs plus clear links to the articles and the handbook as the site's main content, with a plain top navigation bar. (Product Expert answer on root/www; Google 7299563; BHW front-page reports.)
4. **Settle the thin and redirecting subdomain pages.** mystery.alephb.uk's 16-word "has moved" page is a redirect plus thin content, both on Google's list of problems. Once the old-origin save import is no longer needed, retire it or make it a plain 301. Until then, keep it `noindex` and out of any sitemap. Check that compromise.alephb.uk and whatsmysize.alephb.uk carry no near-empty or duplicate language pages. (Google 81904 on redirects; Product Expert on subdomain content; evidence Low but the change is cheap.)
5. **Consolidate rather than add.** Group the articles and handbook into a few clear sections, each with real depth, and merge or noindex any page whose value is mostly duplicated elsewhere (for example near-twin learn pages). The one detailed first-hand approval after 6 refusals came from restructuring, not from writing more. (makemoneyonlineworldwide 2025-06-12; Google 10015918.)
6. **Strengthen "who runs this" without naming a person.** Write a fuller About page saying what AlephB is, why the course material exists, the teaching background behind it, and how the site is run, linked from every page footer, plus a line per article saying who wrote it and why. E-E-A-T weighs less here because SQL is not YMYL. (Community guide; ramrom About page.)
7. **Let the game's prose show, or make sure it sits on a linked article page.** If the game page keeps an ad slot after approval, make sure the screen shows publisher text by default, not only inside a closed `<details>`. The alephb.uk article about the game already covers the review side. (Policy wording; JS-crawl uncertainty.)
8. **Keep growing traffic, but do not treat it as a gate.** Reports range from about 10 to 250 visits a day and contradict each other. It probably helps at the margin. Do not delay a request for it once points 1-6 are done.
